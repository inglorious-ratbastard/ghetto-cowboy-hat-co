##### **About This Repo:**
##### Ghetto Cowboy Hat Co. is a custom streetwear and headwear concept built around the idea of combining traditional Western aesthetics with a modern, unconventional design language.

##### This repository contains the front-end development version of the Ghetto Cowboy Hat Co. business website. The project is designed to provide an immersive storefront experience where customers can explore custom hat designs, browse available products, experiment with different design concepts, and progress through a simulated online ordering experience.

##### The website intentionally moves away from the traditional look and feel of a Western apparel store. Instead, it uses an ultramodern, streetwear-inspired visual identity intended to make the brand feel distinctive, bold, and contemporary while still maintaining recognizable elements of cowboy and Western culture.

##### <ins>Project Goals</ins>:

##### The primary goals of this project are to:

##### Establish a distinctive digital identity for Ghetto Cowboy Hat Co.
##### Showcase custom and limited hat designs in an organized storefront.
##### Provide a foundation for customer-driven product customization.
##### Demonstrate a complete front-end shopping experience.
##### Provide interactive shopping-cart functionality.
##### Create an order and checkout workflow suitable for future expansion.
##### Generate order confirmations and invoice-style information.
##### Maintain a responsive experience across desktop and mobile screen sizes.
##### Provide a foundation that can eventually be connected to a production e-commerce platform or backend service.

##### <ins>Website Experience</ins>:

##### The site is structured as a static front-end business application, with the primary functionality handled through HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

##### Rather than functioning solely as a promotional landing page, the project explores the structure of a small-scale e-commerce application. The interface includes concepts such as:

##### Product presentation
##### Custom customer designs
##### Shopping-cart management
##### Item quantity management
##### Order summaries
##### Checkout-style workflows
##### Order confirmation
##### Invoice/order information
##### Dynamic front-end interactions
##### Responsive page layouts
##### Brand-focused visual presentation

##### The current implementation serves as both a working demonstration and a foundation for continued development.

##### <ins>Custom Design Concept</ins>:

##### A central idea behind Ghetto Cowboy Hat Co. is the ability to move beyond simply purchasing a pre-designed hat.

##### The project explores a customer-focused customization model, where the customer can interact with available design options and envision a product that reflects their own style.

##### This approach is intended to make the website feel less like a conventional catalog and more like a digital design studio for personalized Western-inspired streetwear.
___
##### <ins>Technology</ins>:

##### The project currently uses a lightweight front-end architecture consisting primarily of:

##### HTML5 — page structure and content
##### CSS3 — layout, responsive styling, branding, and visual design
##### JavaScript — interactive functionality and client-side behavior
##### Foundation — responsive front-end framework
##### GitHub Pages — development/demo deployment
##### GoDaddy — production/business website hosting and domain

##### The project deliberately maintains a relatively lightweight architecture, making it easy to develop, test, deploy, and eventually migrate toward a more robust application architecture if required.

##### <ins>Development Status</ins>:

##### This repository represents an active development release.

##### The GitHub Pages deployment provides a development/demo version of the website while the production business presence is maintained separately.

##### As development continues, planned areas of expansion may include additional customization capabilities, improved product management, persistent shopping-cart data, backend order processing, customer accounts, payment integration, and a more complete inventory/order-management system.

##### <ins>Development Philosophy</ins>:

##### The project is being developed with an emphasis on visual identity, usability, experimentation, and extensibility.

##### The goal is not simply to create another online storefront, but to establish a recognizable digital environment for a brand that intentionally sits somewhere between Western heritage, custom craftsmanship, streetwear culture, and modern design.

##### The codebase is therefore treated as an evolving foundation rather than a finished product. Features, layouts, interactions, and underlying architecture may continue to change as the concept develops.
